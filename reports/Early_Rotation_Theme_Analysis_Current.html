Bifurcated Tape: Tech Leadership + Real Asset Rotation Failure
The single fact that explains the tape: Rates have likely stabilized after the Aug spike; capital is rotating back into tech (Cybersecurity +12% on 85% breadth, Software tools accelerating) while simultaneously exiting real-asset hedges (Agriculture now -0.98% 1W after being +8.9% leader in Aug; REITs breaking with only 23% breadth). This is a normalization trade, not a crash, but it has real conviction behind it.

  • Cybersecurity — explosive +12% 1W on 85% breadth after rate-driven Aug selloff. OKTA +28.88%, CRWD +19.76% leading institutional re-entry.
  • Software / Tools — EDA tools (SNPS +16.83%, CDNS +10.83%) and DevOps platforms (FROG +15.95%) showing quality momentum; Data/Analytics sub at +9.99% with 100% breadth.

  • Uranium / Nuclear — +10.49% 1W as energy policy clarity and geopolitical concerns drive new bid (UEC +22.15%, DNN +16.88%).
▼ What Weakened
  • Agriculture / Commodity Ag — complete reversal from Aug. Now -0.98% 1W, -3.48% 1M; grains/oilseeds broke -4.92% 1W. China easing narrative failed.
  • Real Estate & REITs — BROKEN, not pullback. -1.37% 1W on -4.57% 1M with 23% breadth (lowest in survey). Refinancing cost pressures + rate-hedge thesis failing.
  • Traditional Energy — -2.32% 1W (BP -6.20%); oil/gas exploration -4.11%. Geopolitical bid fading.
  • Defense & Aerospace — low breadth warning (34%); RKLB -7.43%, CW -6.60% showing internal deterioration despite 3M +4.58% base.
  • Space Tech — confirmed dead: -4.61% 1W, -38.17% 3M, 33% breadth. Pre-revenue, capital-intensive, off-mandate.
◆ Market Structure Shift

Aug 21 → Now: In August, rising long yields (30Y ~5.27%) were forcing capital OUT of duration assets (semis -8.2%, cloud fading) and INTO hard assets (ag +8.9%, metals +9.9%). Today, rates appear to have stabilized; the rotation is reversing. Capital is moving BACK into defensive tech (cyber, tools infrastructure) and OUT of real-asset hedges that benefited from inflation fears.

This is not a new uptrend in Semis/Cloud — it's a return to normalcy after an overcorrection. Be selective.

▶ Positioning Implications

For a quality/value investor: this rotation offers SELL opportunities in broken themes (REITs, traditional energy, ag) and SELECTIVE BUY opportunities in quality tech infrastructure (SNPS/CDNS/FROG) if daily PPO shows reset structure within positive weekly trends. Avoid chasing Cyber or Uranium into parabolic moves (both are extended). Focus on nibble points with breadth recovery and PPO divergence.
