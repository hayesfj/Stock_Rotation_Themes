01
Executive Summary
The Single Fact

Only one of 34 themes — Energy – Traditional — clears the strict numeric bar for EARLY ROTATION this week. But that headline undersells what's happening underneath: 10 of 175 sub-themes are flagging EARLY ROTATION, and they cluster almost entirely in the hard-asset, power-infrastructure and engineering complex this mandate favors — nuclear/power generation, data-center hardware, auto-supplier engineering, metals recycling and marine/energy logistics — while a long list of software, cybersecurity and consumer-narrative themes are failing outright on breadth. Capital rotated toward tangible infrastructure this week, not toward speculative growth.

What Improved
  • Energy – Traditional: genuine EARLY ROTATION, 73.2% breadth, negative down-day capture (−0.77) — rose on the market's down days.
  • Nuclear/power buildout (GEV, CEG, CCJ) and data-center infrastructure (VRT, NVT, IBM) sub-themes both posted the two highest sub-theme accelerations in the dataset.
  • Auto-supplier engineering names (BWA, ALV, MGA) rotating ahead of the OEMs (Ford, Tesla), which are still broken.
What Weakened
  • 12 of 34 themes registered FAILED ROTATION this week — the broadest breadth failure in this report's history — concentrated in Cybersecurity (18.5% breadth), Business Services (9.1% breadth) and Defense & Aerospace (17.0% breadth).
  • Real Estate & REITs (13.3% breadth) and Consumer Goods & Retail (25.5% breadth) both fell hardest into a rate-sensitive tape after the payrolls beat.
  • Precious metals / gold sub-themes decelerated sharply (accel −6.46pp and −7.55pp) after 1-month gains above +27% — a maturing trade, not a broken one.
Down-Day Behavior & Positioning

Six themes posted negative down-day capture this week (rose on the market's down sessions): Energy – Traditional (−0.77), Commodities – Energy (−0.91), Commodities – Agriculture (−0.61), Agriculture & FoodTech (−1.70), Education Technology (−0.25) and Conglomerates & Diversified Holdings (−0.07). That is the actual defensive-bid list this week — not a proxy from 1-week breadth. On the other side, Space Tech (4.21), Cybersecurity (3.38), Electric Vehicles & Mobility (2.87) and Industrial Automation & Robotics (2.50) all captured more than 2.5x the market's decline on down sessions — high-beta, fragile positioning that sold hardest into weakness. With the Fed decision two weeks out and yields at 52-week highs, we'd rather be positioned in the first list than the second heading into the CPI/PPI prints.
