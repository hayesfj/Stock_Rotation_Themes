01Executive Summary

This was a relief week more than a rotation week: nearly everything that had been sold hard the week before bounced together, and the signal engine moved a cluster of old-economy groups straight from failed to early. Under the confirmation rule the early list is shorter than the engine’s own, because two groups it flagged early on the blend have no early sub-theme beneath them and are reported as constructive mixed. A rebound this broad is encouraging, but it only becomes rotation where the sub-themes and the individual names agree.

  • The week: an indiscriminate rebound in which failure shrank from most of the board to a few corners (electric vehicles, airlines and metals) while most groups improved at once.
  • Rotating in: traditional energy, defense and aerospace, and industrial machinery lead Tier 1, and only energy has names that confirm it from the bottom up; XOM leads the energy majors, with GE and ITW the cap leaders in defense and machinery.
  • Rolling over: big-data analytics lost its mature standing, airlines and metals slid into failure, and a run of former leaders (gold, fertilizer, solar, EV charging) flipped to failed inside a rising tape.
  • Where the risk sits: hardware and semiconductors bounced hard but remain deep in the red over the month, with AAPL and NVDA carrying the cap-weighted numbers, and the electric-vehicle group’s green cap-weighted week is a TSLA story; a weak tape in technology and a hawkish rate backdrop are how this bounce fails.
  • Entry queue: the high-conviction list more than doubled but not one name carries over from last week, and the most populated parents are financials and traditional energy; work only names whose parent group is early, mature or constructive mixed, and treat anything inside a failed group as a probe at most.
