01Executive Summary

This was a relief week more than a rotation week: nearly everything that was sold hard the week before bounced together, and the signal engine promoted a cluster of old-economy groups straight from failure to early rotation. A bounce this broad is encouraging, but it is also what a dead-cat looks like until the monthly picture repairs. The groups worth the work are the ones rising from a flat month, not from a collapsed one.

  • The week: an indiscriminate rebound in which only a handful of groups remain in outright failure and most of the market improved at once.
  • Rotating in: traditional energy, defense and aerospace, and building-and-industrial names lead Tier 1, with digital media and education technology as the quieter additions; think XOM, GE and SHW as the cap leaders.
  • Rolling over: software and big-data analytics slid out of the leadership buckets, and metals, solar, EV and real-estate sub-themes slipped into failure even inside a rising tape.
  • Where the risk sits: semiconductors and hardware bounced hard but remain deep in the red over the month, with NVDA still the largest cap in the group; a weak Friday in technology and a hot rate backdrop are how this bounce fails.
  • Entry queue: the HIGH CONVICTION list more than doubled to 34 names, but not one name carries over from the previous run’s list, so treat it as a fresh queue and work only the names whose parent group is early, mature or constructive mixed.
