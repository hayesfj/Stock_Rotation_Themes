01 — Executive Summary
The Rotation This Week: Duration Out, Hard Assets & Defensives In
The single fact that explains the tape: long-dated Treasury yields spiked to their highest since 2007 (30Y ~5.27%) as US gross debt crossed $40T, and the Treasury announced expanded long-end bond buybacks to contain it. Rising long rates re-priced the crowded, long-duration leaders (semiconductors, AI, cloud, cyber) lower and pushed capital into real assets and defensives. Friday Aug 21 was also August monthly OPEX — read the single-day bounce (Dow +1%, materials +2%, Russell noise) as partly positioning/gamma, not a clean trend signal.
▲ What Improved
  • Agriculture & fertilizer — the cleanest new early-rotation signal. Fertilizer/crop-inputs +8 to +9% on the week off a negative 1-month base; a real fundamental catalyst (China phosphate export curbs, Middle-East supply disruption, firming crop prices into fall application).
  • Metals & materials — gold at a 3-month high, copper & diversified miners extending. Real-asset bid intact.
  • Healthcare — defensive leadership; led the Friday tape (Merck, J&J). Broad (78% of names green).
  • Crypto — bitcoin's best week in two years (+~22%); financials got a lift from crypto names.
▼ What Weakened
  • Semiconductors — a full washout: 0% of names green on the week, cap-weighted −8.2%. The rate-sensitive momentum leader is unwinding.
  • Defense & aerospace — broad −5% pullback (only 12% green) after a strong 3-month run — a reset, not yet a break.
  • Clean energy / smart grid — electrification, solar and wind all lower; grid −9% on the week.
  • Data-center build-out contractors (Comfort Systems, EMCOR, United Rentals) cracked hard — an early tell on AI-capex enthusiasm.
◆ Down-Day / Selloff Test

The week carried genuine down days (Aug 18 & 20) on the bond move. Themes that held or were bought into weakness: Agriculture (75% green), Healthcare (78%), Consumer Staples/Goods (76%), Real Estate (78%), Business Services (86%). Themes that broke: Semis (0%), Defense (12%), Hardware (13%), Renewables (21%), Industrial Automation (22%). Metals looks strong cap-weighted but breadth is only 69% — the strength is narrow (gold/copper carrying it while steel breaks).

▶ Positioning Implications

For a quality/value mandate this is a favorable rotation: money is leaving high-multiple software/AI and entering exactly the balance-sheet-driven, cash-generative real-economy businesses you prefer. Lead with agriculture inputs (early rotation + real catalyst + reasonable valuation). Use the defense/aerospace and industrial pullback as a shopping list, not a warning — 3-month trends there remain intact. Respect crowding in gold (+41% in a month is late-stage). Avoid chasing the semis/AI unwind.
