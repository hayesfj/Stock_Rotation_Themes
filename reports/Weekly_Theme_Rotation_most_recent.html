01Executive Summary

This was a liquidation week, not a rotation week. The sell-off that started in the AI names spread to almost everything, and the signal engine marked every theme but one as failed, with no early sub-theme anywhere in the universe and an empty high-conviction queue. There is nothing to rotate into yet; the job this week is to note what held up and wait for the tape to stop falling.

  • The week: broad, indiscriminate selling led by semiconductors, AI and crypto, with small caps, energy, financials and industrials pulled down alongside them — very few stocks in the universe finished the week higher.
  • Rotating in: nothing. Cybersecurity is the only theme that is not failed, and its green cap-weighted week is PANW and the identity names carrying a group whose typical stock still fell; the platform sub-theme, cattle and protein producers like TSN, and food distribution are the only corners that held near flat.
  • Rolling over: all of last week’s early and mature leaders — traditional energy, defense and aerospace, machinery, the analog-chip bounce — flipped to failed in one week; NVDA lost roughly a quarter of its value and dragged semiconductors and AI to the bottom of the board.
  • Where the risk sits: volatility is at its highest since spring and credit spreads widened every day, so this is a market-wide de-risking rather than a theme-specific break; the equal-weighted picture is as bad as or worse than the cap-weighted one in most groups, so the damage is not just a mega-cap story.
  • Entry queue: empty — no name passed the high-conviction screen against a full queue last week. Do not force entries; the first constructive signal will be breadth recovering above half in groups whose names are mostly still above their long-term trend, such as business services and software.
