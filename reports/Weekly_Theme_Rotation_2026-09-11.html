Executive Summary
The fact that explains the tape: a holiday-shortened week in which WTI pushed above $100 on the US–Iran conflict, August core CPI printed +0.3% month-on-month against +0.2% expected, and the probability of a Federal Reserve rate hike at the 15–16 September meeting repriced from roughly 70% to roughly 90%. The 10-year finished at 4.98% and the 30-year at 5.36%, the highest levels since 2023. The index damage was modest — the S&P 500 lost 0.6% and recovered most of it on Friday — but underneath, breadth collapsed. Twenty-eight of 34 themes now carry a FAILED ROTATION signal and not one theme qualifies as EARLY ROTATION. This was a discount-rate event, not an earnings event.

  • Breadth rose in only six themes. Hardware 58.1→67.5%, Telecommunications 54.8→63.3%, Business Services 9.1→18.2%, Defense 17.0→24.5%, Digital Entertainment 35.6→41.3%, Cybersecurity flat at 18.5%. The first two are the whole Tier 1 list.

  • Five sub-themes still print EARLY ROTATION: Semis Testing & Packaging, Hardware Networking, Semis Analog & Power, Hardware Industrial & IoT, Telecom Satellite. Rotation is visible at the sub-theme layer before it is visible at the theme layer.
What weakened
  • Twenty of 34 themes changed signal; nineteen of those were downgrades. Energy — Traditional fell from the only EARLY ROTATION reading straight to FAILED. All three MATURE LEADERSHIP themes — Healthcare & Biotech, Commodities — Metals and Crypto & Blockchain — went to FAILED in a single run.

  • FinTech breadth of 5.7% and Education Technology at 5.6% are the lowest readings in the book, from 52.8% and 47.1% a week ago. That is liquidation, not rotation.
  • Long-duration software repriced. FDS −13.99% and PTC −7.26% on the week dragged Big Data & Analytics and Industrial Automation respectively.
Down-day results — measured, not inferred

Down-day capture is the theme's return divided by SPY's across SPY's actual down sessions in the trailing window. Six themes printed negative capture — they rose while the market fell: Energy — Traditional (−0.74), Digital Entertainment (−0.60), Commodities — Energy (−0.45), Conglomerates (−0.22), Commodities — Agriculture (−0.16), and Agriculture & FoodTech (−0.87).

Held up during weakness — low capture, breadth intact, trend above the 30-week EMA: Hardware (0.08 / 67.5% / 64.9%) and Telecommunications (0.30 / 63.3% / 63.3%). These are the only two themes that satisfy all three conditions.

Buyable on pullback — high capture this week but positive 3M structure and trend intact: Semiconductors (1.62), Healthcare & Biotech (1.27), Commodities — Metals (2.77), Financials — Traditional (0.82). Different trade, different sizing.
