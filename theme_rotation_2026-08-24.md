## 3 — Tier 1 Themes

### Agriculture & FoodTech + Crop Inputs ⭐⭐⭐
*1W +6.3% · 1M +0.5% · 3M +13.7% | ~$227B wtd · breadth 57%*

**Why it's accelerating:** The fertilizer sub-group put up **+9.2% in a single week after a negative trailing month (−4.2%)** — the classic reset the framework hunts for. The trigger is a nitrogen supply squeeze: urea and ammonia prices jumped on Strait-of-Hormuz supply dislocation, and a weak dollar is lifting globally-priced ag commodities. Precision-ag equipment is riding the same farmer-economics improvement (China resuming U.S. soybean/corn buying, rising global acreage).

**What changed this week:** A month ago, investors were *exiting* fertilizer names expecting prices to ease once the strait reopened — that's what produced the mediocre 1M. Prices didn't ease; nitrogen stayed tight, and the group re-accelerated hard into a market that was selling semis. That divergence (ag up while the tape is down) is the institutional accumulation tell.

**Institutional behavior:** Money rotating out of crowded AI/semis is finding cheap, cash-generative cyclicals. Analyst revisions are turning up (Corteva target lifted to $92, Buy; sell-side lifting CF nitrogen models). Buyers appeared into weakness rather than selling the strength — the group closed near highs on down days.
