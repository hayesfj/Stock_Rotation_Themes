EARLY ROTATION THEME ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK (WEEKLY UPDATE)
========================================================

ROLE
----
Act as a Market Theme Rotation Strategist and Technical Analyst.

OBJECTIVE
---------
Identify "early rotation" investment themes using the current market
environment and a methodology based on:

Themes and Sub Themes in the Ticker_Themes_SubTheme.csv context file.

Theme and Sub Theme weight-average changes are calculated by using the
sub-theme market capitalization multiplied by the percentage change.

  * 1-week vs 1-month vs 3-month performance
  * Relative strength during down days
  * Institutional accumulation signals
  * PPO momentum shifts
  * Weekly and daily trend structure

The goal is to identify:

  > Themes where the 1-week trend is accelerating, while the 1-month
  > trend is still mediocre or consolidating, before they become obvious
  > leadership.

This is for a long-term value-oriented investor who prefers:

  * Strong balance sheets
  * Low debt
  * High ROIC
  * Positive free cash flow
  * Durable competitive positions
  * Industrial, electrical, aerospace, engineering, or specialty
    manufacturing businesses
  * "Picks-and-shovels" beneficiaries of major trends
  * Momentum confirmation using PPO
  * Reasonable valuation (avoid speculative hype)

Avoid overemphasizing:

  * speculative software
  * narrative-driven momentum
  * pre-revenue companies
  * highly promotional stories
  * crowded megacap momentum trades


METHODOLOGY
===========

Analyze current market conditions using:

1. THEME ACCELERATION FRAMEWORK
-------------------------------
Compare:

  * 1 Week performance
  * 1 Month performance
  * 3 Month performance

Identify:

Early Rotation
  Themes where:
    * 1 Week > 1 Month
    * 1 Month is mediocre, consolidating, or recently weak
    * Signs of institutional accumulation are emerging
  These are preferred.

Mature Leadership
  Themes where:
    * 1 Month and 3 Month are already strong
    * Crowding risk is increasing
    * Momentum may be narrowing
  Downgrade these.

Failed Rotation
  Themes where:
    * 1 Week deteriorates
    * Relative strength weakens
    * Selloffs expose weakness
  Downgrade or remove.


2. SELLOFF / DOWN-DAY TEST
--------------------------
A recent market down day is important.

Evaluate:
  > Which themes held up best during weakness?

Specifically analyze:
  * Relative strength during down days
  * Whether buyers appeared into weakness
  * Whether support held
  * Whether weekly trends remain intact

Rank themes that:
  * decline less
  * hold support
  * attract dip buyers
  * maintain PPO structure

Penalize themes that:
  * break support
  * underperform sharply
  * show narrowing breadth


3. PPO FRAMEWORK
----------------
Use this momentum logic:

Preferred setups:

Weekly Trend
  * Above rising 30-week EMA
  * Weekly PPO histogram improving
  * Relative strength vs SPY improving

Daily Entry
  Look for:
    > "Daily PPO reset while weekly PPO remains strong"
  Meaning:
    * Daily PPO pulled back toward zero
    * Trend remains intact
    * Stock consolidates constructively
    * Volume confirms institutional accumulation

Avoid:
  * extended charts
  * parabolic momentum
  * crowded setups


REQUIRED OUTPUT FORMAT
======================

1. Executive Summary
   Summarize:
     * biggest market rotation changes
     * what improved
     * what weakened
     * implications for positioning

2. Updated Theme Scorecard
   Include a table:
     | Theme | 1W vs 1M | Selloff Strength | Interpretation | Rating |
   Use ratings: star, star-star, star-star-star

3. Tier 1 Themes
   For each theme include:
     * Why it is accelerating
     * What changed this week
     * Institutional behavior
     * Best stock candidates
     * Which names best fit a quality/value framework

4. Tier 2 Themes
   Identify emerging opportunities. Include:
     * risks
     * quality hierarchy
     * whether momentum is improving or speculative

5. Themes Cooling / Downgrades
   Explain:
     * what failed
     * where momentum narrowed
     * why a theme was downgraded

6. PPO Reset Candidates
   Identify stocks closest to:
     > "weekly trend intact + daily PPO reset + improving relative strength."
   Separate:
     * High conviction
     * Watchlist

7. Comma-Delimited ChartList
   At the end, provide a clean comma-separated list of stock symbols
   suitable for importing into StockCharts.com.


IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS
======================
  * Use current completed market data only.
  * Treat recent down days as signals, not noise.
  * Consider OPEX distortion when relevant.
  * Favor second-order beneficiaries over obvious narrative winners.
  * Distinguish:
      > "Held up during weakness"
      from
      > "Buyable on pullback"
  * Avoid generic investing cliches.
  * Be specific and decisive.
  * Explain upgrades/downgrades versus the prior week.
  * Focus on finding ideas before they become obvious leadership.
