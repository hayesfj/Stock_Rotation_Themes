01 — Executive Summary
The tape is quietly rotating out of AI/semis into cyclical value
The headline change vs. last month: Agriculture flipped from a sleepy 1-month consolidation (theme +0.5%) into clear 1-week leadership (+6.3%), driven by a nitrogen-fertilizer supply squeeze and a weak dollar — before it shows up on anyone's leaderboard. This is the cleanest “early rotation” signal in the book this week.
What accelerated (1W > 1M)
  • Agriculture & FoodTech +6.3% 1W vs +0.5% 1M — fertilizers & precision ag
  • Commodities–Agriculture +5.0% vs +0.3% — crop inputs re-accelerating
  • Fertilizer sub-group +9.2% 1W after a negative 1M (−4.2%) — textbook reset
  • Quality industrial distributors (GWW, CSX, CTAS) turning up off shallow 1M dips
What broke this week
  • Semiconductors −9.0% 1W, breadth 0% — the epicenter of the selloff (Micron −5.8%)
  • Cybersecurity −7.4% 1W reversing a +3.9% 1M — failed rotation
  • Defense/Aerospace supply chain −6.6% 1W — CRS, CW, HXL, HWM all rolling over
  • Telecom −6.2%, Industrial Automation −4.5% — deteriorating
Mature leadership — hold, don't chase
  • Gold miners still +12% 1W but +33% 1M / +19% 3M — extended, crowding risk (AEM/AU +50% 1M)
  • Healthcare the best down-day performer, now at all-time highs — quality but no longer “early”
  • Software/Cloud +7–11% 1M but decelerating hard into the tape
Positioning implications

Buy the ag-inputs reset on quality (CF, CTVA) and the precision-ag compounders (DE, CNH). Keep a defensive picks-and-shovels core (GWW, CSX, packaging) that held up on down days. Don't chase gold — keep the core, add only on a real pullback. Avoid broken semis and the rolling-over aerospace supply chain until weekly structure repairs.

OPEX note: August monthly expiration was Fri 8/15. The sharp down days on 8/18–8/20 are post-OPEX, so they read as genuine de-risking (rising yields, oil, a 9% Walmart drop) rather than gamma-pin distortion — the relative-strength signals below are cleaner than usual.

03 — Tier 1 Themes
Agriculture: the early rotation that fits a quality/value book
Agriculture & FoodTech + Crop Inputs
1W +6.3% · 1M +0.5% · 3M +13.7%  |  ~$227B wtd · breadth 57%
⭐⭐⭐

Why it's accelerating: The fertilizer sub-group put up +9.2% in a single week after a negative trailing month (−4.2%) — the classic reset the framework hunts for. The trigger is a nitrogen supply squeeze: urea and ammonia prices jumped on Strait-of-Hormuz supply dislocation, and a weak dollar is lifting globally-priced ag commodities. Precision-ag equipment is riding the same farmer-economics improvement (China resuming U.S. soybean/corn buying, rising global acreage).

What changed this week: A month ago, investors were exiting fertilizer names expecting prices to ease once the strait reopened — that's what produced the mediocre 1M. Prices didn't ease; nitrogen stayed tight, and the group re-accelerated hard into a market that was selling semis. That divergence (ag up while the tape is down) is the institutional accumulation tell.

Institutional behavior: Money rotating out of crowded AI/semis is finding cheap, cash-generative cyclicals. Analyst revisions are turning up (Corteva target lifted to $92, Buy; sell-side lifting CF nitrogen models). Buyers appeared into weakness rather than selling the strength — the group closed near highs on down days.
